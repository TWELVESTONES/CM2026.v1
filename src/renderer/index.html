    Add an account

    Google Drive / OneDrive / Dropbox open a browser sign-in window — finish there, then come back. WD My Cloud (and other NAS devices) connect over your local network instead, so that one asks for the device's address and login directly.

    Connect a network drive

    Password is optional only if this share allows guest access. Your WD device's address and share name are visible in WD Discovery or your router's device list.
